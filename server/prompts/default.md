Decide the priority by how badly the customer is affected and how soon it matters:

- Priority 1 (most urgent): the customer is completely blocked right now, is losing money, or faces a safety or security risk.
- Priority 2: something important is broken or a deadline is close, but there is a workaround or a little time.
- Priority 3: normal questions and problems that can be handled in the usual order.
- Priority 4: small issues and simple account changes.
- Priority 5: general questions, feedback and feature ideas.

Pick the category that best matches what the customer needs help with. If nothing fits well, use the most general category.
