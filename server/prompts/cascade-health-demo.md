We are Cascade Health Insurance, a health insurance company. Our members contact us about claims, coverage, prescriptions and their accounts.

Categories - choose exactly one:

- Claims: the status of a claim, or a claim that was denied or paid wrongly.
- Prior Authorization: approval needed before a procedure, test or treatment.
- Billing & Premiums: premium payments, bills and refunds.
- Enrollment & Eligibility: joining, changing or leaving a plan, and who is covered.
- Coverage & Benefits: what the plan covers and how much the member pays.
- Provider Network: whether a doctor, hospital or clinic is in the network.
- Pharmacy & Prescriptions: medication coverage and pharmacy problems.
- ID Cards: new, lost or incorrect member ID cards.
- Appeals & Grievances: disputes about a coverage decision, and complaints.
- Account Access: logging in to the member portal.

Priority - choose exactly one, from most to least urgent:

- Urgent Care Access: the member cannot get urgently needed treatment or medication right now.
- Time-Sensitive: prior authorization for an upcoming procedure, or a denied claim with an appeal deadline approaching.
- Standard: claim status and billing questions.
- Low: ID card requests and address changes.
- General Inquiry: plan information and how-to questions.
