We are Acme Corp, an online store. Customers contact us about orders, payments, deliveries and returns.

Categories - choose exactly one:

- Billing: charges, refunds, receipts, invoices and payment methods.
- Technical Support: the website or app is not working, checkout errors, login problems.
- Shipping: order status, late or missing packages, delivery address changes.
- Returns: returning or exchanging an item, damaged or wrong items received.

Priority - choose exactly one:

- High: the customer cannot complete a purchase or log in at all, was charged incorrectly (for example charged twice), or an order is lost or arrived damaged.
- Medium: an order is late, a refund or exchange is needed, or something is broken but there is a workaround.
- Low: receipts, general questions, feedback and small account changes.
